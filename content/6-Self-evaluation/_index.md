During my internship at **AWS First Cloud AI Journey** from **17/04/2026** to **12/07/2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge I gained at university in a real-world working environment.

I joined **Team WLA** as the **AppSync & Schema Lead** for the **Web Management Task Realtime** project. During the internship, I was responsible for designing and developing the GraphQL schema, building APIs with AWS AppSync, and integrating AWS services such as AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon EventBridge, and AWS SAM to develop a real-time task management system. In addition, I strengthened my skills in system analysis, backend architecture design, AWS infrastructure deployment, and effective collaboration within an Agile development team.

Regarding my working attitude, I always made every effort to complete assigned tasks on time, comply with the team's regulations and development processes, and actively communicate with teammates to improve work efficiency. Throughout the internship, I maintained a positive attitude, accepted feedback constructively, and continuously improved both my technical and professional skills.

### Areas for Improvement

Although I have made significant progress throughout the internship, I believe there are still several areas that I should continue improving to become a more capable software engineer in the future:

* **Enhance initiative and technical contributions:** I would like to become more proactive in proposing technical solutions, contributing new ideas during project development, and participating more actively in technical discussions within the team.

* **Strengthen problem-solving skills:** When working with technologies such as GraphQL, AWS AppSync, AWS Lambda, and other AWS services, I aim to further improve my analytical thinking and troubleshooting abilities so that I can identify root causes and resolve technical issues more efficiently.

* **Improve communication and teamwork:** I plan to develop stronger communication skills by presenting technical ideas more clearly, discussing solutions effectively with teammates, and collaborating more efficiently in an Agile development environment.

* **Continue learning and keeping up with new technologies:** Cloud computing evolves rapidly, so I will continue exploring new AWS services, expanding my knowledge of Serverless Architecture, DevOps practices, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) to enhance my professional expertise.
