>In this section, I would like to share my experience throughout the **First Cloud Journey (AWS Study Group)** internship program and provide some suggestions that may help improve the program for future interns.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working and learning environment at First Cloud Journey is professional, friendly, and highly supportive. Everyone is willing to share knowledge and help each other whenever difficulties arise. The program also encourages self-learning and hands-on practice, which gave me the confidence to explore AWS services and continuously improve my technical skills.

**2. Mentor and Admin Support**  
The mentors were knowledgeable, approachable, and always willing to help whenever I encountered technical challenges. Instead of providing direct answers, they encouraged me to analyze problems independently, read the official AWS documentation, and understand the root cause before finding a solution. The Admin team also responded promptly whenever support was needed, ensuring that the learning process was smooth and uninterrupted.

**3. Relevance to My Major**  
As a Software Engineering student, I found the internship highly relevant to my academic background and career goals. Participating in the **Web Management Task Realtime** project as the **AppSync & Schema Lead** allowed me to apply my programming knowledge while gaining practical experience with AWS cloud services and modern serverless application development.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development**  
Throughout the internship, I had the opportunity to learn and work with various AWS services, including Amazon EC2, Amazon VPC, Amazon S3, Amazon Route 53, AWS Backup, AWS Storage Gateway, Amazon FSx, AWS AppSync, AWS Lambda, and Amazon DynamoDB. Besides technical knowledge, I also improved my ability to read technical documentation, collaborate with teammates, manage tasks, and work effectively in an Agile development environment.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**  
One of the most impressive aspects of the program was the collaborative culture. Team members were always willing to share knowledge, discuss technical solutions, and support one another throughout the learning process. This positive environment motivated me to continuously improve and made me feel like a valuable member of the team.

**6. Internship Policies and Benefits**  
The program provided AWS Credits, comprehensive learning materials, and structured hands-on labs that allowed interns to practice with real AWS services. The learning roadmap was well organized, making it easy to follow each module and gradually build practical cloud computing skills.

### Additional Questions

**• What satisfied you the most during your internship?**  
The most rewarding part of my internship was having the opportunity to participate in the **Web Management Task Realtime** project and apply AWS technologies to a real-world application. Completing hands-on labs and successfully deploying AWS resources significantly increased my confidence in cloud computing.

**• What do you think should be improved for future interns?**  
I believe the program could include more technical workshops, real project sharing sessions, and collaborative coding activities with mentors. These activities would provide additional opportunities for interns to exchange ideas, strengthen practical skills, and learn from real-world experiences.

**• Would you recommend this internship program to your friends? Why?**  
Absolutely. I would highly recommend First Cloud Journey to anyone interested in cloud computing. The program offers a well-structured learning path, practical AWS labs, real project experience, and continuous support from experienced mentors, making it an excellent place to develop both technical and professional skills.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

**• Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
I hope the program can organize more advanced workshops, technical sharing sessions, and cross-team collaboration activities. These experiences would help interns gain broader knowledge and improve their problem-solving and communication skills.

**• Would you like to continue participating in this program in the future?**  
Yes. If given the opportunity, I would be delighted to continue participating in First Cloud Journey, deepen my knowledge of AWS cloud architecture, Serverless technologies, and DevOps practices, and contribute to more real-world projects.

**• Additional Comments:**  
I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the mentors, Admin team, and everyone at First Cloud Journey for their guidance and support throughout the internship. This experience has significantly strengthened both my technical expertise and my professional skills, and it has provided a solid foundation for my future career in cloud computing.
